About JustiPhi Technologies

A Tauranga-based freelance technology business helping people and small businesses make, fix and build things.

Hands-on help across hardware and software

JustiPhi Technologies brings together three things I enjoy doing: 3D printing, fixing computers, and writing software. Instead of juggling separate specialists, you get one person who can design and print a replacement part, sort out a slow desktop PC, or build the tool your business has been missing.

Based in Tauranga, I work with individuals, hobbyists and small businesses around the Bay of Plenty, and I keep things simple. You'll get a straight answer about what's possible, a clear quote before any work starts, and regular updates until the job is done.

With both FDM and resin printers available, I can match the process to the part, whether it's strong enough to use every day or detailed enough to put on display.

At a glance

  • FDM and resin 3D printing
  • 3D modelling and prototyping
  • Desktop PC repair and IT support
  • Websites, custom software and databases
  • Based in Tauranga, New Zealand
