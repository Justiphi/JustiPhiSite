About JustiPhi Technologies

A Tauranga-based freelance business building software for 3D printer owners and offering hands-on 3D printing services.

Software and services for 3D printing

JustiPhi Technologies brings together two things I enjoy: 3D printing and writing software. My current focus is Layer Zero, a tool for keeping track of printer maintenance, repairs and spare parts, built from the problems I've run into keeping my own printers going.

Based in Tauranga, I work with individuals, hobbyists and small businesses around the Bay of Plenty, and I keep things simple. You'll get a straight answer about what's possible, a clear quote before any work starts, and regular updates until the job is done.

With both FDM and resin printers available, I can match the process to the part, whether it's strong enough to use every day or detailed enough to put on display.

JustiPhi Technologies is run part-time, so I work outside standard business hours: weekday evenings and weekends.
